# P1383 Adventure Works Sales Analysis

An end-to-end sales analytics project completed using **Excel, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and PowerPoint** to analyze sales performance, profitability, products, customers, regions, and time-based trends.

## Project Overview

The project uses the **Adventure Works sales dataset** and develops separate analytical deliverables across Excel, SQL + Tableau, and Power BI.

The analysis focuses on:

- Sales and profit performance
- Year-wise and month-wise trends
- Quarter-wise sales and profit
- Regional performance
- Product performance
- Customer analysis
- KPI reporting
- Interactive dashboard development
- Business recommendations

## Key Findings

### Sales Trends

2013 recorded the highest annual sales at approximately **$16.35M**, compared with approximately **$7.08M in 2011** and **$5.84M in 2012**.

### Quarterly Performance

**Q4** contributed the highest share of quarterly sales at approximately **31%**, followed by **Q3 at 26%**.

### Monthly Performance

**December** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **$3.21M**, while **February** recorded the lowest at approximately **$1.74M**.

### Regional Performance

**Australia** was the largest regional contributor at approximately **$9.06M**, followed by **Southwest at approximately $5.72M**.

### Product Performance

**Mountain-200** and **Road-150** variants were among the leading products based on sales and profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing products.
- Investigate differences in sales performance across regions.
- Prepare inventory and resources ahead of high-sales periods, particularly Q4 and December.
- Evaluate products using both sales and profit rather than sales alone.
- Use customer-level analysis to support retention and customer-value initiatives.
